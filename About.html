About us

CookScript is a powerful recipe blogging platform created by Vishant Netke and Vinayak Kausadikar, designed to unite food lovers, home cooks, and bloggers. With a clean interface and robust features, it allows users to easily share their favorite recipes, explore a variety of cuisines, and connect with a vibrant community of culinary enthusiasts. Unlike its earlier no-database version, CookScript now uses a fully integrated database system, enabling seamless storage and management of user profiles, recipe data, categories, comments, and likes. This upgrade ensures faster access, better scalability, and a more personalized experience.

Users can create accounts, save their favorite recipes, and even engage with others by commenting and rating dishes. Whether you're an experienced chef or just experimenting in the kitchen, CookScript makes it easy to document and share your creations. The platform is fully responsive, ensuring a smooth experience on desktops, tablets, and mobiles. Advanced search filters help users quickly find what they're craving—be it vegan meals, desserts, or regional specialties. If you're passionate about cooking and blogging, CookScript is the ultimate destination. Join us and be part of a growing food-loving community, where every recipe tells a story and every dish is celebrated.
